How to use GesturePad

Gestures

  • Open palm (hold) → new tab
  • Swipe left / right (open hand, quick horizontal move) → history back / forward
  • Peace (hold) → duplicate tab
  • Thumbs up (hold) → reload tab
  • Point → air pointer on the stage
  • Pinch → grab & drag the orb on the stage
  • Fist (hold) → stop / cancel

Live control

By default gestures only drive the on-screen playground (safe mode). Flip LIVE CONTROL to execute real browser commands on the active tab of your focused window. GesturePad never acts on its own tab, and it cannot close tabs — by design.

Tips

Keep your whole hand in frame, palm toward the camera, ~40–80 cm away. Good lighting helps. Static poses must be held steady (tunable) — swipes fire instantly. One action per pose entry: change your hand shape to re-arm.

Honest limits

Geometry-based recognition can confuse unusual hand angles; very fast swipes may be missed at low FPS; background tabs are throttled by the browser to ~1 Hz. This is a convenience tool, not an accessibility guarantee.

Privacy

All tracking runs locally in WASM (MediaPipe). No video, landmarks or gestures ever leave your machine. Only tuning is stored.
